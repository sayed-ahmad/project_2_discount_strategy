# 📊 Project 2: Discount Strategy Analysis

## 📌 Overview

This project focuses on analyzing whether offering discounts is a beneficial strategy for a company. The analysis covers product pricing, discount patterns, and seasonal effects to provide data-driven business recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

To determine **whether discounting products positively impacts business performance** and to provide insights into how discounts should be strategically applied.

---

## 🧠 About the Project

In this project, I worked as a data analyst to:

* Clean and validate the dataset by addressing data quality issues
* Perform exploratory data analysis to uncover trends and patterns
* Generate actionable insights related to discount strategies
* Present findings to support decision-making at the management level

---

## 📈 Business Questions Addressed

This analysis provides clarity on the following:

* How products can be classified into meaningful categories for better reporting and analysis
* Distribution of product prices across different categories
* Proportion of products being discounted
* Magnitude of discounts as a percentage of product prices
* Impact of seasonality and special events (e.g., Christmas, Black Friday) on sales
* Recommendations for improving data collection processes

## 📊 Outcome

The project delivers insights into:
